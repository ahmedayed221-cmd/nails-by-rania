About

The hands behind the work

I'm Rania, a nail artist working at the intersection of fine detail and everyday craft — hand-painted sets, sculpted 3D florals, and the close-up photography and process reels that document them. Every set on this site started as a client brief and ended up as something I was proud to shoot close up.

This portfolio is intentionally minimal: dark background, high-contrast type, and a grid that puts the work first. Filter by category above, or open any piece for the full write-up.
